# Generation prompt

Built-in image generation was asked for one exact 4 x 4 production sprite sheet of
the same human dark-fantasy traveler in every frame. Rows were specified as south,
east, north, and west. Columns were specified as idle, left-leg walk contact,
right-leg walk contact, and one-handed sword attack.

The character specification required short dark hair, dark padded leather armor,
two visible armored shoulders and articulated arms, separate boots, a weathered short
cloak, one short iron sword, and a muted ochre protagonist accent. The camera was a
fixed orthographic mostly-overhead view from the south with shallow pitch, explicitly
excluding an isometric diamond, horizon, ground tile, and vanishing point.

The animation constraints required alternating legs and counter-swinging arms during
walking, a clearly extended weapon arm during attacks, identical identity/equipment/
scale across all frames, matching feet anchors, genuine transparent gutters, and no
labels, grid lines, UI, background, extra characters, or watermark.
