# Generation prompts

The project-bound raster assets were created with the built-in image-generation
tool. The production request for both characters was a modular 4 x 4 cutout atlas,
not an animation sheet or complete-character pose.

## Human hero

Create sixteen isolated painted dark-fantasy body and equipment parts on genuine
transparent alpha. Use a classic top-down action-RPG view with a gentle
three-quarter overhead angle baked into the art, keeping the head, shoulders,
chest, arms, pelvis, thighs, lower legs, and boots readable. Arrange head
south/north and torso south/north in row 1; neutral straight upper arm, forearm and
hand, thigh, and shin and boot in row 2; armor south/north, pauldron, and helmet in
row 3; sword, shield, belt, and ochre cape in row 4. Keep joints vertically aligned
with predictable top-center and bottom-center anchors. Avoid complete characters,
animation frames, extreme foreshortening, backgrounds, grids, labels, text, and
watermarks.

## Goblin raider

Use the same camera, layout, anchors, scale discipline, transparent gutters, and
painted rendering as the human atlas, but create an original compact olive-green
goblin with wide ears, ragged leather, wrapped limbs, clawed hands and feet, a
knife, hide buckler, rope belt, and back cloth. Preserve straight neutral limb
pieces and avoid human anatomy, full-character poses, backgrounds, grids, labels,
text, and watermarks.

## Goblin transparency correction

Remove only the generated gray-and-white checkerboard and replace it with genuine
transparent alpha. Preserve all sixteen pieces, the exact 4 x 4 positions, canvas
proportions, scale, color, linework, joint anchors, and gutters. Produce clean dark
antialiased contours without a white or gray fringe.
