## 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: six extremely simple DARK PAPER-CUT game-pawn sprite candidates on one square review atlas, 3 columns by 2 rows, equally sized cells, generous empty margins, no labels or grid.
Design goal: the minimal readable pawn economy of a top-down colony simulator, NOT elongated character concept illustrations. Original dark fairy-tale art. Each character is a compact squat ONE-AND-A-HALF-HEADS-TALL paper game token: head 45% of silhouette height, broad tapered coat/body 45%, TWO tiny close-set boots peeking below the hem 10%. Body must be complete and rounded/angled closed at bottom, never a cropped bust. No visible leg length. No arms sticking out; arms integrated into coat silhouette or absent. Width at least 65% of height. All face forward, slightly overhead character-symbol view for an orthographic top-down game, not isometric.
Six identities left-to-right: human traveler, elf, dwarf; orc, wolf beastkin, human innkeeper. Human: one angular swept hair chunk, ash-bone tunic, one small asymmetric rust shoulder fold. Elf: two narrow pointed ears and a pale swept hair wedge, muted teal compact cloak. Dwarf: broad squarish head, one single rust beard trapezoid, squat grey apron body. Orc: wide gray-green jaw and two tiny bone tusks, charcoal rounded tunic with rust diagonal band. Wolf: two sharp ears, one ash muzzle triangle, compact slate tunic and two paw tips. Innkeeper: simple bone headscarf, faded plum dress/apron silhouette, two little shoe tips. Complete bodies, no detailed human anatomy.
Style: visibly hand-cut irregular angular paper silhouettes, 4-6 BIG SHAPES PER CHARACTER, flat fills only, two values plus one small accent per character. Soot black contour defined by a slightly offset dark backing-paper silhouette, NOT thick rounded cartoon sticker outlines. Large unmarked faces without any eyes, mouths or expressions. No gradients, no 3D bevel, no soft shading, no texture/grain, no fine strokes, no buckles, no fingers, no knees, no necklaces, no weapons.
Mood: weary, quiet and eerie, not cheerful or cute mascot art, no smiles or skull decoration. Palette ash #B0ADA0, bone #D8CEB4, charcoal #262B2F, rust #8C493A, dusty teal #567875, very limited faded plum. Characters lighter than background. Uniform flat dark blue-slate #414C55 background, no environment, no shadow, no checkerboard, no fake alpha.
The six figures must read from their coarse silhouettes at just 28-36 screen pixels in an 18x18-tile phone viewport. Use unusually economical geometry, NOT polished generic fantasy icon rendering.
