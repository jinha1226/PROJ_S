## 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: six SIMPLE DARK PAPER-CUT top-down town-building sprite candidates on one square sheet, 3 columns by 2 rows, each separate with generous padding.
Art direction: original dark fairy-tale settlement built from flat hand-cut paper shapes; the extremely economical readable design of a colony simulation. Mostly soot-charcoal, ash-grey, faded bone, tiny rusty red and dull amber accents. Subdued desaturated colors. Characters would appear lighter than these buildings. Small warm windows/hearths imply shelter in a bleak world.
ABSOLUTE SIMPLICITY: each building consists of ONLY 6-10 major flat polygons, one or two LARGE representative functional objects. No roof shingles, bricks, cobblestones, plank lines, woodgrain, paper texture, sketches, tiny decorative clutter, plants, ropes, signage, hatch shading, gradients, drop shadows, glossy icons or realistic rendering. Shape edge irregularity supplies the paper-cut character, not surface texture. Flatten detail as though designed to read at only 64-96 screen pixels.
Camera MUST be straight vertically down, axis aligned roof plans on a flat tile map, not isometric, no tilted view, no facade illustrations. Buildings complete within equal cells, no surrounding land-base or background squares. Genuine transparent background, no checkerboard painted into image. No labels or text or grid.
Reading order:
1 INN: compact rust gabled roof drawn as two broad roof-plane polygons, single charcoal square chimney, little open ash courtyard with one bone long table and two black benches.
2 MARKET: L-shaped charcoal roof with two large bone rectangular cloth awnings, one rusty crate shape.
3 HEALING HOUSE: simple pale ash octagonal roof surrounding an open dark square, one dull turquoise rectangular healing pool inside and two large bone bed shapes.
4 SMITHY: angular charcoal roof, one broad rust chimney with tiny amber opening, one open side bay containing a single large pale anvil silhouette.
5 STOREHOUSE: plain broad ash rectangular roof, dark loading edge and two broad rust crate blocks.
6 EXPEDITION LODGE: asymmetric charcoal roof with one large faded bone canvas extension, an open forecourt with one bone table bearing a single rust triangular map marker.
Large clearly distinct silhouettes, weathered shapes with one chipped roof corner at most, no ruin collapse. Town is functioning, restrained warmth, not haunted Halloween props or cute toy houses. Keep all six graphically as simple as abstract paper game pieces.
