# Monster detail study — not selected for runtime

Built-in imagegen. The output is more detailed and more side-facing than approved player art. Preserve as a study; use a simplified follow-up for runtime.

Four small fantasy game creatures in a 2x2 sprite sheet, evenly spaced on solid bright magenta background for color keying. Row one: a green goblin in a simple moss-colored tunic, an orange kobold in a gray tunic. Row two: a turquoise slime, a dark blue-gray beetle with six short legs. Whole creatures visible, compact proportions, no weapons. Simple traditional low-resolution 24-pixel game-sprite design shown enlarged, chunky square pixels, crisp dark outline, 8-12 muted colors per sprite. Goblin and kobold have big distinctive heads and tiny close-set feet; slime and beetle have their own animal shapes. Quiet dark-fantasy colors with clearly readable light midtones. No text, no grid lines, no shadows, no complex texture.
